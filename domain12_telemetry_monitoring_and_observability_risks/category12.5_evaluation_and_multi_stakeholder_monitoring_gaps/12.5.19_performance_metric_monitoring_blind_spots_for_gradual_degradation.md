# 12.5.19 Performance Metric Monitoring Blind Spots for Gradual Degradation [NoFW]

Per-agent performance metrics are monitored independently; coordinated attacks that degrade multiple agents' calibration simultaneously by small amounts create compound quality degradation that exceeds acceptable system-level bounds while no single agent's individual degradation exceeds its per-agent alert threshold.

For example, an attacker who coordinates a 0.8% calibration degradation in each of twelve agents simultaneously produces a system-level calibration failure of nearly 10% through compounding, while each individual agent's monitoring sees only its own 0.8% movement and does not trigger its 2% alert threshold.

Standard defenses such as per-agent calibration monitoring and statistical process control on individual agent metrics are insufficient because they evaluate each agent's performance independently and lack a mechanism to detect that simultaneous sub-threshold degradation across all agents constitutes a coordinated attack rather than normal variance.

**Multi-agent distinction:** Single-agent gradual degradation eventually accumulates in one agent's metrics until alerts fire; multi-agent coordinated degradation distributes the attack across independent monitoring domains so that no individual agent's monitoring ever sees a signal large enough to alert, while the compound system-level effect remains entirely invisible to every stakeholder.
