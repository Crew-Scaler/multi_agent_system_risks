# 12.5.37 Reasoning Quality Drift in Production Without Continuous Monitoring [NoFW]

Reasoning quality evaluation is performed pre-deployment but not maintained as a continuous production metric; gradual reasoning quality degradation from model updates, prompt drift, or distribution shift goes undetected until it manifests as user-facing failures, and in multi-agent systems this degradation cascades through downstream agents, amplifying its impact beyond what single-agent reasoning drift would produce.

For example, a summarization agent evaluated at deployment for coherence and factual consistency receives a model update six weeks later that introduces subtle hallucinations in complex reasoning chains; because no continuous reasoning quality monitoring is in place, the degradation propagates through a downstream recommendation agent that treats the summarization outputs as ground truth, compounding reasoning errors without any alert triggering until customer complaints surface months later.

Standard defenses such as pre-deployment evaluation, model versioning, and A/B testing are insufficient because they establish point-in-time quality baselines rather than continuous monitoring, and prompt drift, data distribution shifts, and model updates that occur after deployment can degrade reasoning quality progressively without crossing discrete alarm thresholds.

**Multi-agent distinction:** In single-agent systems, reasoning quality drift affects only that agent's outputs; in multi-agent pipelines, reasoning quality degradation in an upstream agent propagates to all downstream agents as their inputs degrade, creating a multiplicative quality decay that is invisible to per-agent monitoring because each downstream agent processes degraded inputs without any comparison to what correct inputs would look like.
