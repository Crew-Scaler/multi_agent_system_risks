# 12.5.25 Tool Response Quality Metrics Poisoning Through Aggregation Bias [NoFW]

Multi-agent systems aggregate tool quality metrics (success rates, latency) across agents; adversaries compromise a subset of agents to report inflated quality metrics, skewing aggregate metrics that drive tool retirement and selection decisions toward attacker-preferred outcomes while individual per-agent quality analysis would reveal the manipulation.

Standard defenses such as aggregate tool quality dashboards and periodic quality audits are insufficient because they evaluate tool health at the system level without per-agent disaggregation, and inflated reports from compromised agents shift aggregated metrics in ways that pass system-level quality checks while hiding the manipulation within the aggregate.

**Multi-agent distinction:** Single-agent tool quality monitoring directly reflects that agent's tool interactions; multi-agent aggregation creates a collective quality signal that can be gamed by a subset of compromised agents, because the honest agents' legitimate tool quality metrics dilute—and thereby conceal—the inflated reports from compromised agents, making the aggregate appear healthier than any individual agent's true performance would indicate.
